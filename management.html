How the SAP Controls Execution Academy was designed, built and delivered

One management reference for product objectives, scope boundaries, two realistic fictional projects, 50 modules, instructor-supported delivery, workpaper review, evidence architecture and the 20-job market benchmark.

Explore architecture

1. Product definition and design decisions

The core product trains SAP Controls, Compliance and Audit professionals to perform risk-based scoping, SOX/ITGC and business-process control testing, evidence validation, exception management, remediation and Controls Lead review. Cross-functional Security, GRC, Basis, cloud and functional topics are taught to an assurance/evaluation level—not as specialist implementation certification.

Three proficiency levels

L1 — Understand

Describe the specialist workstream, terminology and controls risks.

L2 — Evaluate

Inspect specialist evidence, challenge conclusions and assess risks.

L3 — Execute

Independently perform Controls work, produce workpapers and defend findings.

Scope rule: L3 applies to Controls, Compliance and Audit execution; most cross-functional topics target L2. The Academy is not an SAP configuration qualification and its fictional simulator is not connected to live SAP.

2. Realistic project architecture

Thornfield · 24 modules

Post-merger controls assurance: inherited baselines, organisational scope, P2P walkthroughs, SoD conflicts, mitigating reviews, change and operations ITGC, access remediation, IPE, user reviews, firefighter logs, R2R controls, security assurance, GRC AC, independent evidence and Lead review.

Open Academy modules

Corvane · 26 modules

Continuous project engagement: landscape inventory, interfaces, risk-to-control scoping, evidence and populations, leaver risk, ITAC/ITGC dependencies, payment controls, defects, coverage, privileged access, HANA, SOC/CUEC, migration, analytics, PC/CCM, S/4 cutover, cloud identity and O2C/payroll/treasury.

Open Academy modules

Scenario design method

Each case connects a fictional business event to a risk, control objective, SAP-oriented procedure, selection criteria, sample evidence, expected workpaper, challenge question, deficiency decision and remediation handoff. The case records and simulated outputs are fictional and must be distinguished from actual client evidence.

3. Delivery model and learner journey

Instructor-led guidance is paired with structured individual practice. The instructor demonstrates the approach, acts as the Controls Lead during review and challenges conclusions; trainees perform the analyst work themselves.

01

Learn the control objective and business risk

02

Study the fictional engagement and SAP-oriented procedure

03

Execute the guided simulation or authorized SAP exercise

04

Capture and reconcile evidence in a structured workpaper

05

Submit for assigned Controls Lead review and revise if requested

06

Defend the conclusion, escalation and remediation recommendation

4. Cross-functional design enhancements

Six enhancements are embedded in existing modules, each with a specialist/Controls responsibility boundary, scenario, evidence list, challenge questions, assessed deliverable and escalation owner. Independent IPE reinforcement is also included in TF-23.

A · TF-21, CV-24

Security architecture and authorization assurance

Evaluate PFCG, SUIM, traces, effective access, role approvals and Fiori control evidence—not build roles.

B · TF-22, CV-23

GRC Access Control and Process Control assurance

Challenge SoD rulesets, ARA/ARM/BRM/EAM, monitoring rules, source completeness and exception resolution—not configure GRC.

C · CV-25

Cloud identity, BTP and RISE controls

Trace joiner/mover/leaver events, IAM reconciliation, cloud entitlements, SOC/CUEC and shared responsibility.

D · CV-24

S/4HANA transformation and cutover assurance

Evaluate migration role mapping, SoD regression, cutover sign-offs and hypercare evidence.

E · CV-26

Business process workstream awareness

Understand O2C, payroll and treasury; independently test their controls without becoming a functional configurator.

F · TF-24

Cross-functional Controls Lead coordination

Plan work, review junior evidence, manage RACI, issue escalation, audit challenge and steering reporting.
