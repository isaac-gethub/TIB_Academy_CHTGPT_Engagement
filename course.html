Two projects. One professional work cycle.

Thornfield · 24 modules

Post-merger Controls assurance

Investigate inherited controls, SoD conflicts, mitigating reviews, access approvals, ITGC operations, change management, invoice and journal controls, evidence quality and Lead decisions.

Corvane · 26 modules

End-to-end Controls engagement

Understand the system landscape, scope risks, assess leaver controls, validate report populations, test payment controls, document exceptions and evaluate S/4, cloud and business-process risks.

Example: A leaver is locked in SAP but retains downstream cloud access. Which control failed, and who must resolve it?

Built with current job requirements in view

Our curriculum was compared with 20 SAP-related job advertisements, including SOX/ITGC, SAP IT Audit, Controls Lead, GRC compliance and Security Controls governance roles. Eight were identified as primary Controls-focused benchmarks. The others inform cross-functional awareness and help us avoid overstating specialist qualifications.

Controls Senior / Consultant

Execute control testing, validate evidence, document findings and support audit requests.

IT Audit / SOX Compliance

Understand risk and control objectives, assess IPE and evaluate design and operating effectiveness.

Controls Lead / Manager

Review workpapers, coordinate workstreams, manage issues and defend conclusions.

Job advertisements were used for curriculum comparison, not as employer endorsements or promises of hiring.

Learn how to challenge evidence—not simply accept a green status

Sample classroom challenge

“Security says the access conflict is fixed.”

You receive an access-risk report, a revised role matrix and a closed ticket. Your task is to determine whether the population is complete, the approvals are valid, the residual access is acceptable and the finding can genuinely be closed.

Your deliverable: A documented Controls conclusion with evidence references, unresolved questions and a remediation decision. The instructor plays the Controls Lead and challenges your reasoning.
