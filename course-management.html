Course architecture

12 instructional weeks; 36 two-hour live sessions per cohort; $950 per trainee. Authorized SAP training-system access is guaranteed, included and mandatory. Cohort 1 meets Monday, Wednesday and Friday; Cohort 2 Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Both meet 6–8 PM ET.

Each session: theory 30 minutes, live instructor SAP demonstration 35 minutes, trainee hands-on SAP exercise 45 minutes, review and assignment 10 minutes.

Training progression: theory → instructor demonstration → trainee exercise → workpaper submission → instructor evidence review → Lead/Manager decision → remediation and feedback → final interview defence.

Delivery governance and release controls
